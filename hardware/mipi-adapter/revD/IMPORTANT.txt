MIPI ADAPTER REV D

Open mipi_adapter_revD.kicad_pro in KiCad 10.

POWER
VBAT remains externally supplied at TP1 / VBAT_IN, with common ground at TP2 / GND.
Keep the external VBAT feed within the panel operating range, including transients (4.5 V maximum in the supplied panel specification).
U1 switches this external VBAT feed to the panel.
U2 switches the existing host 3.3 V feed to VCI_PANEL through R2.
The two input supplies are separate. Both TPS22917 ON pins share PANEL_EN.

MANUAL JUMPERS
R1: VDDIO / 1.8 V connection.
R2: host 3.3 V connection to U2's VCI input.
R4: panel reset connection.
R5: ties shared PANEL_EN to host 3.3 V for automatic enabling.
These remain DNP in the BOM, matching the original manual-jumper assembly approach.
For an external enable signal at TP7 / EN, leave R5 open/remove it and actively drive EN high or low.
Do not drive TP7 externally while R5 ties it to the host supply.

COMPONENTS
U1 and U2: TPS22917DBVR.
C7 and C10: 10 nF CT-to-VIN timing capacitors, each referencing its own input supply.
C8 and C9: 1 uF input bypass capacitors for U1 and U2 respectively.
C1-C6 retain their original values.

DSI/MIPI routing, connector pin mapping, board stackup and impedance rules are unchanged.
Both switches share an enable, but independently supplied rails do not have a guaranteed relative startup order.
PCB DRC/connectivity/parity pass. Hardware behavior has not been measured.
